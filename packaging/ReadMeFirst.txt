Semantic Nets -- a typed semantic network editor, with a reasoner.

@REQUIREMENTS@

To install: drag "Semantic Nets" onto the Applications folder.

The first time you open it: this demo isn't signed by an identified Apple
developer, so macOS will refuse to open it with a double-click. Instead,
Control-click (or right-click) "Semantic Nets" in Applications, choose Open,
and then Open again in the dialog. After that it opens normally.
(Or: System Settings > Privacy & Security > "Open Anyway".)

On its first launch it puts some example models in
  Documents/Semantic Nets Examples
and opens them. The Help menu has the User Guide.

Its settings are kept in ~/.sn.xml, and its log in ~/Library/Logs/Semantic Nets.
