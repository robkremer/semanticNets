Families
--------
  
  - An ontology model for families, constraining genders of mothers and fathers, and 
    defining ancestors, descendents, full- and half- siblings, brothers, and sisters,
    grandparents, and children.
    `https://robkremer.github.io/semanticNets/models/families/familyOntology.snm <https://robkremer.github.io/semanticNets/models/families/familyOntology.snm>`__
    
    - A view of the above:
      `https://robkremer.github.io/semanticNets/models/families/familyOntology.snv <https://robkremer.github.io/semanticNets/models/families/familyOntology.snv>`__
  
  - A model of the Flintstone family (and friends) from the cartoon, together with a number
    of example queries:
    `https://robkremer.github.io/semanticNets/models/families/Flintstones.snm <https://robkremer.github.io/semanticNets/models/families/Flintstones.snm>`__
    
    - A view of the above:
      `https://robkremer.github.io/semanticNets/models/families/Flintstones.snv <https://robkremer.github.io/semanticNets/models/families/Flintstones.snv>`__
