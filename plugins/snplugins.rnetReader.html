snplugins.rnetReader package¶

rnetReader: a plugin (see sn.plugins) that makes a model and view of a Rep Plus net (a “.rnet” file: Brian Gaines’s CNet and KNet), from the Tools menu’s “Rep Plus Net Reader…”.

  • rnet: reads the file;

  • reader: makes the model;

  • hooks: a KNet node’s truth marks – their bars, and its menu’s items to mark it (and to show its net’s probes, to work on its net as a whole, to expand it, and to abduce from it);

  • probes: a net’s probes, and the links they say hold;

  • forms: a net as a whole: its canonical forms, saturating it with its probes, and merging nets;

  • expansion: a net redrawn: its protoconnectives expanded, anonymous nodes added, and its links factored;

  • existence: the senses in which each of a net’s nodes exists;

  • abduction: KNet’s abductive inference: what holds whatever explains a node;

  • view: the front end, and the view.

Created on Nov 11, 2026

Code author: Rob Kremer <robkremer@me.com>

snplugins.rnetReader._dialog: Any = None¶

The dialog, while it’s open: the menu item brings it to the front rather than opening another.

snplugins.rnetReader.register(ctx)¶

Add “Rep Plus Net Reader…” to the Tools menu (see sn.plugins.PluginContext): its dialog (see view.ReaderDialog) asks for the net file – in the folder last read from – and the model and view to make of it: by default in the folder they were last made in (else beside the net), and makes them (see view.convert()).

Parameters:

ctx (sn.plugins.PluginContext) – The plugin’s context.

Return type:

None
