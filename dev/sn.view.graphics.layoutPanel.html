sn.view.graphics.layoutPanel module¶

The layout panel: a pane at the right of a view’s window (beside the canvas; its divider can be dragged) for trying layouts out without going through the menus. Shown and hidden with “View | Layout panel” (see SNGView.showLayoutPanel()), or its own close button; dragged narrower than LayoutSplit.CLOSE_WIDTH, it closes. While it’s hidden, a narrow strip at the window’s right edge opens it again – clicked, or dragged out (see LayoutSplit). Its top has

  • a switch between the three kinds of layout (see layout.CATEGORIES): “arrange now” (static), “keep arranged” (dynamic) and “animate”;

  • for the first two, a list of the layouts of that kind (the one keeping the view arranged is ticked); and

  • a bar of buttons for what’s shown, which never scrolls away: for the first two, Revert (back to the values the layout was last used with) and Arrange (arrange the view once, as one undoable command) or Use (make it the view’s dynamic layout); for “animate”, the animation layout’s start/stop button and what it’s doing.

Below that is a page for the chosen layout: its parameters (the form its dialog shows: see layoutDialog.LayoutForm) – or, for “animate”, the animation layout’s settings (see animationControls), which take effect at once. Edits not yet used are kept while other pages are shown.

The page is made afresh each time it’s chosen, so its lists of relation types are the model’s as they are then.

class sn.view.graphics.layoutPanel.LayoutSplit(view)¶

Bases: PanedWindow

A view’s canvas and, beside it at the right, its layout panel – or, while that’s hidden, a narrow strip (STRIP_WIDTH) that opens it: clicked, or dragged out wider than OPEN_WIDTH. The panel dragged narrower than CLOSE_WIDTH closes; narrower than LayoutPanel.MIN_WIDTH, it springs back to that. It takes the canvas’s place in the window’s AppPanedWidget (above the terminal).

Parameters:

view (SNGView)

STRIP_WIDTH = 14¶

How wide (pixels) the strip is.

OPEN_WIDTH = 60¶

The strip dragged out wider than this (pixels) opens the panel.

The panel dragged narrower than this (pixels) closes.
