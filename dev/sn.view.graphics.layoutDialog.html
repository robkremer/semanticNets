sn.view.graphics.layoutDialog module¶

The dialog that asks for a layout heuristic’s parameters (see layout.LayoutHeuristic.askParameters()): made from its PARAMETERS (see layout.LayoutParameter) – a relation type, a table of them (see RelationTableEditor), a choice, a number, or a yes/no – with OK and Cancel. The form itself (LayoutForm) is also what the layout panel’s pages show (see layoutPanel).

Created on Oct 10, 2026

Code author: Rob Kremer <robkremer@me.com>

sn.view.graphics.layoutDialog.MAX_INT = 10000¶

The biggest number the dialog’s number fields take.

class sn.view.graphics.layoutDialog.RelationTableEditor(parent, table, model, shown, compact=False)¶

Bases: Frame

Edits a relation table (see relationTable.RelationTable) as a tree of relation types (see relationTable.relationTree()): those shown in the view, and their supertypes, each subtype indented under its supertype (▸ and ▾ close and open a supertype: it’s closed unless one of its subtypes has a row of its own), and the relation types’ own ends (see relationTable.TERMINALS). Each has a role (see CHOICES): its own row’s, or – “as <supertype>” – the nearest supertype’s that has a row (a row stands for its subtypes: so one for a supertype can do for all of them). ↑ and ↓ move a type among its siblings (with its subtypes): the higher rows win. Compact (eg: in the layout panel), each is on two lines: its name, and below it its role.

Parameters:

  • parent (tk.Misc)

  • table (RelationTable)

  • model (Any)

  • shown (list[Any])

  • compact (bool)

INDENT = 12¶

How far (pixels) a subtype’s indented under its supertype.

CHOICES: dict[str, tuple[str, str]] = {'ignored': ('ignored', ''), 'parent: destination': ('shapes the tree', 'destination'), 'parent: source': ('shapes the tree', 'source'), 'pulls together': ('pulls together', '')}¶

The roles a type’s own row can have: the choice, and its role and parent end (“parent: …”: it shapes the trees, with that end the parent).

TIPS = {'down': 'Move it down, with its subtypes: the higher rows win.', 'open': 'Show or hide its subtypes.', 'role': 'Its role: shapes the trees (with which end is the parent), pulls things together, or is ignored -- or, "as" one of its supertypes, whatever that one\'s row does.', 'up': 'Move it up, with its subtypes: the higher rows win (eg: for a node related to two parents).'}¶
