sn.view.graphics.imageExport module¶

Pictures of what’s on the canvas: a PDF drawn from the canvas’s own items (rectangles, ovals, polygons, lines and text – what Shape, Decorator and the connectors draw), and a PNG made from it, for the clipboard (see copyImage()): eg: to paste a rule into Messages or Preview.

Tk can only draw on the screen, and macOS no longer converts the PostScript a Tk canvas can write, so the items are drawn again here, as PDF: a vector picture, with no need for a screen capture (and its permission), of everything asked for, on screen or not. Text is set in PDF’s own Helvetica, which is very close to the canvas’s Arial; a few symbols it hasn’t (eg: the arrows in names) are taken from PDF’s Symbol font, and a window’s collapse triangle is drawn. The PNG is made from the PDF at twice the size (marked 144 dpi, so it’s shown the same size: sharp on a Retina screen) – by macOS’s sips; elsewhere, by the pypdfium2 package – and put on the clipboard (see util.platformSupport.pdfToPNG(), copyPNG()).

Space (canvas pixels) left around what’s drawn.

How much bigger than the canvas the PNG is drawn (see the module’s description).

sn.view.graphics.imageExport.SYMBOLS = {'×': 180, '←': 172, '↑': 173, '→': 174, '↓': 175, '⇐': 220, '⇒': 222, '∀': 34, '∃': 36, '∈': 206, '∞': 165, '∧': 217, '∨': 218, '≠': 185, '≤': 163, '≥': 179}¶

Characters not in Helvetica that PDF’s Symbol font has: its codes for them.

sn.view.graphics.imageExport.TRIANGLES = {'▲': 'up', '▴': 'up', '▶': 'right', '▸': 'right', '▼': 'down', '▾': 'down', '◀': 'left', '◂': 'left'}¶

Characters drawn as triangles (eg: a context window’s collapse toggle).

sn.view.graphics.imageExport.FONTS = {(False, False): 'Helvetica', (False, True): 'Helvetica-Oblique', (True, False): 'Helvetica-Bold', (True, True): 'Helvetica-BoldOblique'}¶

PDF’s fonts for (bold, italic).
